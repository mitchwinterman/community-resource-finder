Community Resource Finder

Quick tips for getting the most out of the directory.

How to use this tool

1. Start with a broad search

Use the search box to type keywords like food, housing, transportation, or a specific organization name.

2. Narrow by category

Use the Category dropdown to focus on a general area such as Housing, Healthcare, or Legal Services.

3. Narrow by subcategory

Once you pick a category, the Subcategory dropdown will show more specific options (for example, “Food Pantries” within Food & Nutrition).

4. View full details

Click on any result in the left-hand list to see full information on the right side: address, contact info, hours, eligibility, and notes.

5. Reset if you get stuck

Click the Reset button to clear filters and start over.

Need more help?

Library staff are happy to help you search for resources or contact organizations. Visit your nearest Washoe County Library branch or use the Contact page to send us a question.
