Community Resource Finder

A Washoe County Library project connecting patrons with local services.

About Community Resource Finder

Community Resource Finder is a tool created by Washoe County Library System to help patrons quickly locate local services, organizations, and programs.

The directory focuses on practical, everyday needs: housing and shelter, food assistance, healthcare, mental health, employment, legal help, transportation, and more.

How we build this directory

Library staff collect information from trusted community partners, verify details, and organize listings into searchable categories and subcategories. Information is updated on an ongoing basis, but programs and availability can change at any time.

Please contact organizations directly to confirm hours, eligibility, and current services.

Limitations & disclaimers

  • This tool is for information only and is not a crisis or emergency service.
  • Inclusion does not equal endorsement, and omission does not mean a service does not exist.
  • If you notice incorrect information, please let us know using the Contact page.
